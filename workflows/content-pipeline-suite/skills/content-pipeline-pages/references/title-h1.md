# SEO title and H1

Hardwired for every service page. Do not invent a third pattern.

## SEO / meta title (algorithms)

For service-area businesses, **omit** the location or area modifier. Format is exactly:

`[Service Name] | [Company Name]`

This is a strict **entity association statement** (semantic couple): service entity + brand entity. Nothing else.

| Do | Do not |
|----|--------|
| `Emergency Tree Service \| Ridgeline Tree Care` | `Emergency Tree Service in Greater Denver \| Ridgeline Tree Care` |
| Title Case service name from the offering / seed | Taglines, “best”, “affordable”, extra modifiers |
| Company name as the public brand | Legal LLC suffix unless that is the public brand |

Write this for search systems. Location belongs in the H1, meta description, and body, not in the title.

## H1 (humans)

Always **restate**, do not **repeat**, the service named in the SEO title.

Use a close variant of that service. You may introduce the service area here (Greater Denver, the city, etc.). Word the H1 like a Google Ads headline: relevant to the service and compelling.

- Still 7th–8th grade. Still succinct.
- Reinforce the same service entity. Do not switch offerings.
- Matrix terms may appear when they fit and the evidence grid allows the claim.

**Repeat (forbidden):** the title’s service string copied forward, with only a location or a tiny add-on (`in Greater Denver`, `for trees`, `Service`).

**Restate (required):** different wording that a visitor would still recognize as the same job.

| SEO title | Fail (repeat) | Pass (restate) |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| `Emergency Tree Service \| Ridgeline Tree Care` | Emergency Tree Service in Greater Denver | 24 Hour Emergency Response Tree Services in Greater Denver |
| `Arborist Services \| Ridgeline Tree Care` | Arborist Services in Greater Denver | Arborist Consultations and Tree Health Assessments in Greater Denver |
| `Deep Root Fertilization \| Ridgeline Tree Care` | Deep Root Fertilization for Trees in Greater Denver | Tree Fertilization and Root Feeding in Greater Denver |

Check before you lock the H1: strip the company name and the service area from both strings. If what is left is still the SEO service name (plus `for`, `Service`, or another one-word tack-on), rewrite.

If “24 hour” / “24/7” is used, the body must keep the evidence-grid meaning. When the grid says that claim covers an answering line rather than guaranteed arrival, the body has to say the narrower thing.

## Where it lands

| Stage | What to write |
|-------|----------------|
| Brief | Plan the human H1 (Ads-style restatement). Note the SEO title as `[Service] \| [Company]` only. |
| Draft / edit | Use that human H1. If the brief H1 is a repeat, rewrite it here per this file. |
| Polish | Technical suite gets the strict SEO title. Top-of-page Markdown and HTML open with the human H1. |
