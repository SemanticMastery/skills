# Reading level (general market)

Write visitor-facing page copy for a **7th–8th grade** reading level. The reader is a homeowner or business owner, not an SEO or arborist.

Applies to draft, edit, polish body copy, FAQ answers, H1, and meta description. The SEO / meta title follows [title-h1.md](title-h1.md) (entity association), not grade-level prose. The brief stays planning-only (no body), but later stages honor this target.

## Target

- Plain English a general adult can read once.
- Short sentences. One idea at a time when you can.
- Common words. Contractions are fine.
- If a Matrix trigger word is technical, keep the term and say what it means in the next clause.

## Do not

- Do not write academic, legal, or brochure-speak.
- Do not pad with extra words to sound “professional.”
- Do not drop a required Matrix term just because it is longer. Keep the term. Explain it.
- Do not simplify a fact into something untrue. The evidence grid still wins.
- Do not narrate omissions to the visitor. Honor Unknown/Omit by silence, then a next step (call, free estimate). Do not write “this page is not…”, “we are not walking you through…”, or “we will not invent…”. Those lines belong in the brief omissions list and the editorial log only.
- Do not run a readability scorer unless the operator asks. Write to the target. Edit by ear.

## Edit check

On the edit pass, read the page out loud. If a sentence needs a second pass, split it or swap the hard word. Note that check in the editorial log.
