# Tree-care industry seed

Ask, never assert. Every line is a candidate to *ask about*. None of these are facts about a campaign until the owner says so.

Use this vocabulary to word Describe and Verify questions. Do not copy a method, mix, brand, cadence, or credential into Product-Documentation or visitor copy from this file.

## Company-wide (ask, never assert)

- License type / number the owner will stand behind (ask, never assert)
- Insurance facts the owner will publish (ask, never assert)
- Tenure / "serving since" line when sources disagree (ask, never assert)
- Certified arborist: owner credential vs subcontract (ask, never assert)
- Eco-friendly / organic as a real standard vs marketing language (ask, never assert)
- Service-area edge and any trip / minimum charge (ask, never assert)
- Warranty or guarantee on any service (ask, never assert)
- Maintenance plans and land clearing: still sold or off the site (ask, never assert)
- Typical crew size and storm-surge limit (ask, never assert)
- Photos or on-site steps that may be described (ask, never assert)
- Who besides the owner may answer (standing question; ask, never assert)

## Deep root fertilization (ask, never assert)

- Rig / equipment brand (ask, never assert)
- House mix vs named product vs mixed per tree (ask, never assert)

- Cadence and Greater Denver seasonal window (ask, never assert)
- Aftercare / watering instructions actually given (ask, never assert)
- Exclusions: lawn fertilizing, disease treatment, oak wilt (ask, never assert)
- Quote basis: per tree, trunk size, property; publishable range (ask, never assert)
