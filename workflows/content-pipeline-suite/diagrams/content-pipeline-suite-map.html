Three sibling interviews — facts, stories, voice

6 · owner-interview

When the page evidence gate returns block. Gaps → pack → capture → compile through product-documentation → re-sync. Facts. Never visitor copy.

7 · expert-interview

After PD exists, on a calendar. Stable /e/ link on the same voice host. Stories land in {Company}-story-bank.md. A pd_candidate is a handoff, not a PD write.

8 · voice-interview

When draft needs a spoken-source Voice DNA. One /v/ link, same Worker, retell/voice. Lands {author}-voice-dna.json. Does not write copy, facts, or stories.

Read the arrows as gates, not suggestions: nothing downstream starts until the thing to its left is genuinely finished.
