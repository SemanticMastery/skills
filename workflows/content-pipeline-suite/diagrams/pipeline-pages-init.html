The three buckets, and why leftovers matter

both

A live URL and a catalog offering. Default include — this is the page you rewrite.

crawl-only

A live page with no offering behind it. Usually a retired service or a stray URL. Skipping it is often correct.

catalog-only

An offering with no page yet. This is new revenue you can launch — or a service they stopped selling. Only the owner knows.

Seeds are derived

Slug or offering name, plus  service unless the last word is already service, services, treatment, response, plan, or plans.

Two stops on purpose: Matrix runs cost time and hit a real login, so nothing runs until the page list and the seed table are both yours.
Out of scope: page copy, blog produce, images, CMS push, location pages
