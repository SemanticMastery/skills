Rules that are not negotiable

SEO title

[Service Name] | [Company Name]. No location, no taglines, no “best”. It is an entity association statement for algorithms.

H1 restates

A close variant a visitor still recognizes, written like a Google Ads headline. The service area is allowed. Copying the title and bolting on a city is a fail.

FAQ answers

Answer the question first. Name the company second. The phone number is last, never the whole answer.

Terms never beat evidence

A trigger word inside a sentence is still a claim. If the grid says Unknown, the term does not make it true.

Blog vs pages: a blog post is picked from the roadmap by post number; a page is picked from the approved catalog by slug.
